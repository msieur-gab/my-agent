## The situation

When a parent is separated from their child by distance, legal disputes or borders, staying emotionally connected becomes nearly impossible. Calls get blocked, messages get intercepted, and whoever controls the channel controls the relationship.

## What everyone assumed

Stay in touch through video calls and messaging apps.

## The reframe

A call needs both people free, and allowed, at the same moment. A child's need for a parent doesn't keep a schedule. Earlier attempts with logbooks, photos and text all failed the same way: they needed literacy and felt like documentation. Not image, not video, not text. Voice carries the message, the feeling and the presence.

## What got built

Hand-formed stones that look exactly like ordinary stones, each with an NFC tag sealed inside. Tap it with a phone and it plays a parent's voice. The audio is encrypted, holding the stone is the key, an optional password protects it further, and messages can be locked until a date: a birthday, New Year, an 18th birthday.

## What changed

The first version relied on IPFS, and when that storage degraded, Pebbble 2 was rebuilt from what the first one taught: a more human stone (an owner card so it can be returned if lost, voices addable from far away) without weakening the security.
