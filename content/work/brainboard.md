## The situation

Knowledge piles up in notes, articles and chats, and gets lost. AI assistants promise to find it again, but they answer confidently whether or not they know.

## What everyone assumed

That a bigger, smarter model is the fix.

## The reframe

A tool you can trust is one that admits what it doesn't know. Small, local and honest beats large and confident for remembering what you already learned.

## What got built

A touch-first thinking canvas. You think spatially with notes, questions, tables and documents. A small local "librarian" retrieves passages from your own writing, always with their source, and abstains when nothing fits. When the thinking is done, you export a clean, cited brief to hand to a frontier model to build.

## What changed

The small model organises the thinking, the big model is only called to execute. Nothing leaves the device until you decide to send the brief.

*This site works the same way: it only answers from my work.*
