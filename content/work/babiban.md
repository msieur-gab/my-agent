## The situation

A father far from his young daughters, with little time together. When they want him and he can't be reached, there's nothing of him in their world.

## What everyone assumed

Give the child an AI voice assistant, or a chatbot that sounds like a parent.

## The reframe

The first thing she hears must be genuinely his. Real words, in his voice. A generated sentence, however good, isn't him.

## What got built

A small device on a Raspberry Pi Zero that listens, understands what a child means (kids phrase things softly and unpredictably), and answers with a phrase her father authored, in his cloned voice, with real recordings for the precious lines. Several replies per intent, so it never says the same thing twice.

## What changed

The language model only chooses, it never writes. Understanding is delegated to a machine, but every word stays human.
