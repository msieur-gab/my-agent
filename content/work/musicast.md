## The situation

I cancelled a music subscription and found my own speakers locked behind it. When I downloaded my 100 GB library, every tag had been stripped: music that had forgotten its own name.

## What everyone assumed

That smart music means a neural network that is always right, and an account to run it.

## The reframe

Legibility is the design. Not a system that is always right, but a system you can always read.

## What got built

A server that casts straight to the speakers, with no account. Fourteen readable formula classifiers (sad, contemplative, hypnotic, radiant…) chosen over neural models. Every track's emotional profile is written into the audio file itself: the database is a cache, the tag is the truth. It was first built on a dead 2017 Chromebook, because the constraint becomes the guarantee.

## What changed

The music, the metadata and the logic all belong to the listener again.
