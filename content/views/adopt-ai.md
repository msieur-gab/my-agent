Don't start with AI. Start with your company's collective intelligence: ask each person which part of their work bothers them most, the part that needs clear steps and decisions rather than expertise.

Write down the rules that move that task forward. Hand them to an agent, test it on anonymised cases from easy to hard, and keep every decision traceable and explainable.

That is a first pilot, and it's yours. If you want a method to stay independent while you grow it, tell me where you are.
