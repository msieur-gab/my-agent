# Views

Short answers to common questions that no project or note of mine covers yet. One file per question.

- `answers:` lists the ways a visitor might ask it, in their words.
- `keywords:` are the words that belong to this question only. Leave out broad ones ("ai", "data"): they pull in questions that a project answers better.
- The text is the reply, two or three short paragraphs.
- `invite:` (optional) is the link that opens the form to write to me, in words that follow from the
  text. Without it the link reads "Tell me about your situation".
- `next:` (optional) lists the views that naturally follow, by file name. Each is offered under the reply
  as a question to tap, worded as the first line of that view's `answers:`.

A view has no page of its own. On the home page it is introduced as a view, not as work, and it ends on
the invitation to talk.
