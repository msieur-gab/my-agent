It depends on what you are trying to solve. AI is a wide roof, from prediction to generation, and the two don't fit the same problems.

Turning photographed invoices into data your business logic can use is a real case. It is worth a pilot, even a small model of your own, for efficiency, cost and auditability.

Automating a flow that already runs on clear, predictable steps is different: it rarely needs a generative layer. Use a frontier model to build the bricks, not to run the flow. That is where you gain control and independence.
