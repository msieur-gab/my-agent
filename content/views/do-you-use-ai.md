Yes. I use AI from different providers, depending on the task: summarising data, visualising it, prototyping fast, sometimes preparing the training of a custom model.

It gets me through the analysis faster. Framing the problem stays mine, and so does every decision that touches people's privacy and agency.

Client data never goes into these tools. When I need data to work with, I build a synthetic set from the patterns I observed.

This site works the same way. A small model runs inside your browser to understand your question and find the closest answer. The answers are mine; no model writes them. One model to interpret, a human to answer.
