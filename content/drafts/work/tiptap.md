## The situation

When a child is taken to another country, they often lose their first language. Within months or years they think, dream and feel in a language their other parent doesn't speak.

## What everyone assumed

Machine translation solves it.

## The reframe

Machine translation gets the words right and the feeling wrong. The message that makes a parent cry in English should make their child cry in Mandarin.

## What got built

A small library of messages, professionally translated by people to keep their emotional weight. Pick one, tap, and it's in your clipboard, ready for whatever app reaches your child. It works offline and nothing leaves the device.

## What changed

The tool disappears in the emotional moment: copy, paste, done. Fewer messages, better translations.
