This is the stance under everything I build.

**Empower.** Give people a real capability, not a hosted convenience they rent.

**Privacy.** Enforced by architecture, not by policy. If an app can't hold your data, it can't leak it, sell it or lose it.

**Restore agency.** Not "respect" agency, which is passive. Restore it: give back the ownership the current web took. The user owns the store; the app only writes into it.

It makes concrete decisions. I think about who owns the data before I think about collecting anything. A central server isn't rejected for its cost but because it makes me a custodian. The values pick the technology, not the other way round.

The goal is not to serve people from a place I control. It's to give them a capability they own.
