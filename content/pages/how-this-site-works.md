---
title: How this site works
---

The home page is a conversation. You ask a question, or pick a theme, and the site answers with a piece of my work.

## Where the answers come from

Only from my own projects and notes. Nothing is generated: every sentence the page says is one I wrote, as a reply to a question I expect. The page compares your question with those, by meaning, and says the reply of the closest one. When the match is loose, it says "this is the closest I have". When nothing fits, it says so and invites you to send me the question, so I can answer it myself.

The comparison is done by a small embedding model, 23 MB, that your browser downloads from this site and runs itself. No request goes anywhere else with your words in it. It's a working demonstration that useful, honest interaction doesn't need a frontier model behind it.

## What is stored

- **No cookies.** Nothing is stored on your device, beyond your browser's ordinary cache of the page and the model.
- **Theme clicks** are counted at most once per visit, with no identifier attached. It tells me which topics interest people.
- **Typed questions** are kept as text only, after names and contact details have been removed in your browser. No IP address, no fingerprint, nothing else. I read them to learn what to write next, then delete them.
- **Messages you send me** include the email address you choose to give, so I can reply. You see exactly what I'll receive before you send it.

## Why

Because this is how I think products should treat people. If a page can't hold your data, it can't leak it.
