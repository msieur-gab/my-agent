---
title: How this site works
---

The home page is a conversation. You ask a question, or pick a theme, and the site answers with a piece of my work.

## Where the answers come from

Only from my own projects and notes. Nothing is generated: the page compares your question with what I have written and shows you my own paragraph that is closest to it. When nothing fits, it says so and invites you to send me the question, so I can answer it myself.

The comparing is done by a small language-understanding model (23 MB). Your browser downloads it from this site the first time you click into the question field, and it runs on your own device: your question is not sent anywhere to be understood. The model only measures how close your words are to mine. It never writes a sentence. If it cannot load, a simpler keyword match takes over.

It's a working demonstration that useful, honest interaction doesn't need a frontier model behind it.

## What is stored

- **No cookies.** The only thing your browser keeps is the model file, in its cache, so it isn't downloaded twice. It identifies nothing and no one.
- **Theme clicks** are counted at most once per visit, with no identifier attached. It tells me which topics interest people.
- **Typed questions** are kept as text only, after names and contact details have been removed in your browser. No IP address, no fingerprint, nothing else. I read them to learn what to write next, then delete them.
- **Messages you send me** include the email address you choose to give, so I can reply. You see exactly what I'll receive before you send it.

## Why

Because this is how I think products should treat people. If a page can't hold your data, it can't leak it.
