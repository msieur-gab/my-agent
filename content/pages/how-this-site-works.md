---
title: How this site works
---

The home page is a conversation. You ask a question, or pick a theme, and the site answers with a piece of my work.

## Where the answers come from

Only from my own projects and notes. Nothing is generated: the page matches your question against what I've written and shows you the closest piece. When nothing fits, it says so and invites you to send me the question, so I can answer it myself.

This is a small, one-purpose language model in spirit, and today a simple matching file in practice. It's a working demonstration that useful, honest interaction doesn't need a frontier model behind it.

## What is stored

- **No cookies.** Nothing is stored on your device.
- **Theme clicks** are counted at most once per visit, with no identifier attached. It tells me which topics interest people.
- **Typed questions** are kept as text only, after names and contact details have been removed in your browser. No IP address, no fingerprint, nothing else. I read them to learn what to write next, then delete them.
- **Messages you send me** include the email address you choose to give, so I can reply. You see exactly what I'll receive before you send it.

## Why

Because this is how I think products should treat people. If a page can't hold your data, it can't leak it.
