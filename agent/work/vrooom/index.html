Vrooom

The brief

“Kids' games belong on screens.”

The question nobody asked

What if the digital part only existed to send parents and kids outside together?

The answer

A hand-planed wooden car with an NFC tag. Tap it at a playground or a walk to check in and earn a badge, then print it, colour it and glue it into a handmade travel passport. The phone witnesses the adventure, it doesn't run it.

The situation

Kids need reasons to explore, and parents need reasons to be there with them. Most digital play pulls both indoors and onto separate screens.

What everyone assumed

Digital play means screen play, with rewards inside an app.

The reframe

The moment spent together is the real reward. Any reward that doesn't involve sharing a moment, being creative or using your hands is refused.

What got built

A hand-planed wooden car with an NFC tag. On walks and at playgrounds, tap to check in and unlock a badge. Badges are printed, coloured, cut out and glued into a handmade passport, so a summer's adventures end up as a paper object the child made, not a profile page. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking.

What changed

Screen time in seconds per interaction: check in, pocket the phone, keep playing together. It's the same idea as Pebbble turned around, from preserving memories to creating them.
