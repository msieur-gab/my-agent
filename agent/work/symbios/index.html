Symbios

The brief

“Your data is safe in our cloud.”

The question nobody asked

What if your computer had no body, and your identity never touched a server?

The answer

A pocket-sized computer that becomes a whole desktop on any screen it's plugged into, with your music, reading, notes and work, fully offline. Your identity is born on your phone and recovered with 16 words, with no company in the loop.

The situation

Every service wants an account, and every account is a copy of you on someone else's server, waiting to leak.

What everyone assumed

Security is the provider's job: trust the cloud and its policies.

The reframe

If there's nothing on a server, there's nothing to leak or breach. Own a computer that is only yours.

What got built

A small single-board computer with no screen of its own. Plug it into a TV, a laptop or a phone and it becomes a full desktop with your music, reading, notes and work, offline-first. The identity is created on the phone and never touches a server: 16 words recover it.

What changed

Unplug it and everything leaves with you. No cloud, no sign-up, nothing to lease.
