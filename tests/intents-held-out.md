# Held-out sentences for the intent model

Written 21 October 2026 by Claude, after training, and not used to tune content/intents.md.
None of these lines is in the training file. `node tests/check.js` reads it.
Same format as content/intents.md: the heading is the reply the sentence should get.
`problem` and `out_of_scope` sentences must not get a written reply (the work, or "no answer", decides).

## greeting
- hey gabriel
- good day
- hello hello
- hi again

## thanks
- thanks so much
- that's great, thank you
- merci gab

## who
- so who are you exactly
- who's behind this page
- what's your profession

## how
- is a human writing these answers
- are these answers generated
- is there an ai behind this
- how are you answering me

## what
- what kind of questions work here
- what can i ask you about
- i'm not sure what to type

## contact
- how do i get in touch with you
- i'd like to discuss a project
- can we meet

## list
- show me everything you have done
- let me see your projects
- what's in your portfolio

## more
- show me another project
- anything more

## where
- in which city do you work
- do you work with clients outside europe
- could you work from our office in paris

## languages
- can we do the project in german
- is your french good
- how many languages do you know

## rate
- what are your fees
- how much is a day of your time
- is a project with you expensive

## availability
- can you start in january
- do you have capacity this quarter
- would you consider a permanent job

## background
- what's your work history
- did you work for big companies
- where were you before freelancing

## process
- how do you approach a new brief
- do you prototype in code
- what do we get when the project ends

## services
- can you do user interviews for us
- do you do visual identity
- could you train our designers

## why
- why would we pick you
- what's your proudest piece of work
- what keeps you busy right now

## problem
- my team wants to try ideas but every test needs approval
- we can't explain our ai to our auditors
- i live in a different country than my kids
- our document requests get lost between departments
- we want to know which systems hold personal data
- how can our product work offline without an account
- we don't have developers, can we still prototype
- our chatbot gives confident wrong answers
- the language barrier with my child worries me
- what does it cost to run an experiment like senz
- how long did the dowgo project take

## out_of_scope
- what's the score of the game tonight
- how do i make pancakes
- what is the tallest mountain
- recommend me a podcast
- how do i reset my iphone
